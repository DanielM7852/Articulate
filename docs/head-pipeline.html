Head match

What happens when you press Hear head, then Say head. The cue is chosen from the two recordings. Claude only rewrites that cue. The table is saved afterward.

1. Hear head

The browser asks Cartesia to say “head”. The same words drive the mouth so the face speaks with that voice.

↓
2. Say head

The microphone records you as a WAV and posts it to /v1/word-match.

↓
3a. Parselmouth on you

Every 10 ms: time, pitch, F1, F2. A frame counts only when pitch is present. Voiced time is that count times 10 ms.

3b. Parselmouth on Cartesia

The saved Cartesia WAV of the word “head” is measured the same way. That WAV is the reference, not the picture of the mouth.

↓
4. One cue, from the numbers

The server compares the two tables. Pitch is kept and is not used for the cue. Claude is not asked to pick the cue.

2970 ms your voiced time · 297 frames × 10 ms
F1 574.3 Hz · F2 1710.6 Hz · pitch 142.8 Hz
160 ms reference voiced time · 16 frames × 10 ms
F1 689.7 Hz · F2 1976.8 Hz · pitch 182.7 Hz

2970 / 160 is much longer than 65%, so “finish the word” is skipped. F1 is 16.7% lower, past the 15% bar, so the cue is mouth_more_open. F2 is only 13.5% lower, so there is no tongue cue.

↓
5. Claude phrases that cue

Claude may rewrite “open your mouth a little more.” If the rewrite adds another body part or the opposite move, the rewrite is dropped and the measured sentence is spoken.

↓
6a. SpacetimeDB, when connected

The table is written by record_articulation into learner_articulation. Raw audio is not stored there. The head screen does not read this table back to decide the cue.

6b. Local file, this run

The 2970 ms take is in backend/.local_store/learners/…json. That means this comparison saved locally. Spacetime was not required for the cue or the sentence.

↓
7. Advice back on the mouth

The screen shows the cue name, “Claude phrased it”, and the two voiced times. The mouth speaks the sentence.
